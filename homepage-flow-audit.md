# Homepage flow audit — 2026-09-08

## Verdict

The individual sections are visually strong, but the middle of the homepage feels awkward because several full-height modules repeat the same pale editorial surface and Kanha is introduced twice in succession: first as the active item in the expedition calendar, then again as the signature cinematic feature.

## Current sequence

1. Hero — strong emotional opening.
2. Ways to journey — clear commercial orientation, but tall.
3. Wildest places — good brand positioning, but visually too similar to the previous light section.
4. Species-led stories — strong destination discovery.
5. Upcoming photo expeditions — useful, but dense and begins with Kanha.
6. Signature Kanha feature — visually distinctive, but repeats the same destination and image immediately.
7. Conservation commitment — credible closing proof, with an abrupt light transition after Kanha.
8. Planner/contact — correct final conversion step.

## Recommended sequence

1. Hero
2. Ways to journey
3. Wildest places
4. Species-led storytelling
5. Signature Kanha expedition
6. Other upcoming expeditions (exclude Kanha from this homepage list)
7. Conservation commitment
8. Journey planner/contact

## Highest-impact changes

- Move Signature Kanha before the expedition calendar.
- Exclude Kanha from the homepage calendar and rename it “Other upcoming expeditions”. Kanha remains available on the full expedition page.
- Reduce the calendar to three compact departures; it should support the signature story rather than compete with it.
- Give Wildest Places a subtly darker limestone surface so the early light sections do not visually merge.
- Shorten Ways to Journey and Wildest Places by roughly 15–20% to improve scroll pace.
- Add a narrow warm transition statement between the expedition content and CSR rather than another full section.

## Evidence limits

The review covers the visible desktop scroll sequence and section hierarchy. Keyboard navigation, screen-reader order, mobile browser rendering and performance timings require separate checks.
